Our platform

StrataDFIR. Forensics that stay on the workstation.

StrataDFIR is our native forensic platform for macOS and iOS. Its language model runs on the device, so you can search, summarise and question evidence on an air-gapped workstation. Nothing is sent to a cloud service.

In development · early-access briefings

  • Built on The Sleuth KitFile-system and volume analysis on a proven open-source forensic core.
  • On-device language modelApple Foundation Models runs locally to help examiners query and summarise artefacts, with no network connection needed.
  • Hash-chained examiner journalEvery examiner action is recorded in a chained log, so later changes to the record are evident.
  • Case lifecycleCases move through defined states from intake to closure, so it is always clear what stage the work is at.
  • Native on Mac and iPhoneBuilt in SwiftUI for macOS and iOS, the platforms we investigate every day.

Approach

Evidence first. Plain English. Next action.

We separate what we saw from what we infer. Every conclusion states its confidence, its limits and the step that would confirm or refute it.

  1. Call

    A short triage call. We tell you what to preserve before anything else changes.

  2. Scope

    We agree the questions to answer, the evidence needed, and handling under TLP and confidentiality.

  3. Analyse

    Evidence is hashed on intake and every examiner step is journaled, so the work can be repeated.

  4. Report

    Findings with confidence levels, indicators, and prioritised actions for your team and regulators.

Dealing with an incident now?

Email security@patata.mt with “INCIDENT” in the subject, a callback number and one line on what you are seeing. Do not attach evidence or passwords to the first message.

  1. Isolate affected machines from the network, but leave them powered on.
  2. Do not wipe, reimage or run cleanup tools yet.
  3. Write down what you noticed, when, and who has touched what.
